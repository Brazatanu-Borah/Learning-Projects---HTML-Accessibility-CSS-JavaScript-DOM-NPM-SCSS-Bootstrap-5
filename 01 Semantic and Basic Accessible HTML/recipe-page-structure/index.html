Classic Beef Lasagna

A hearty, layered Italian classic with rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and perfectly baked pasta. This recipe serves 8 and takes about 2 hours from start to finish.

Ingredients

For the Meat Sauce

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 carrots, finely chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, finely chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 500 g (1.1 lb) ground beef
  • 200 g (7 oz) ground pork
  • 800 g (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 cup dry red wine (or more broth)
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp dried basil
  • Salt & black pepper to taste

For the Béchamel Sauce

  • 100 g (3.5 oz) unsalted butter
  • 100 g (3.5 oz) all-purpose flour
  • 1 L (4 cups) whole milk, warm
  • 1 tsp ground nutmeg
  • Salt & white pepper to taste

For Assembly

  • 375 g (13 oz) lasagna noodles (about 12-15 sheets)
  • 400 g (14 oz) mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 150 g (5.3 oz) Parmesan cheese, grated
  • Fresh basil leaves for garnish (optional)

Step-by-Step Instructions

Prepare the Meat Sauce

  1. Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat.
  2. Add onion, carrots, and celery; cook until softened, about 8-10 minutes.
  3. Stir in garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add ground beef and pork; cook, breaking up with a spoon, until browned, about 10 minutes.
  5. Pour in red wine (or broth) and scrape any browned bits from the bottom.
  6. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, beef broth, oregano, and basil.
  7. Season with salt and pepper; bring to a simmer.
  8. Reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 45-60 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Make the Béchamel

  1. In a medium saucepan, melt butter over medium heat.
  2. Whisk in flour and cook for 1-2 minutes until golden and bubbling.
  3. Gradually pour in warm milk, whisking constantly to prevent lumps.
  4. Cook, stirring, until the sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon, about 8-10 minutes.
  5. Season with nutmeg, salt, and white pepper.
  6. Remove from heat and set aside.

Cook the Noodles

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions until al dente.
  3. Drain and lay the noodles flat on a baking sheet to prevent sticking.

Assemble & Bake

  1. Preheat oven to 190 °C (375 °F).
  2. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce over the bottom of a 9×13 inch baking dish.
  3. Arrange a single layer of noodles over the sauce.
  4. Spread one-third of the remaining meat sauce over the noodles.
  5. Top with one-third of the béchamel, then sprinkle with mozzarella and Parmesan.
  6. Repeat layers (noodles, meat sauce, béchamel, cheeses) two more times, finishing with a layer of cheese.
  7. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  8. Remove foil and bake for an additional 20-25 minutes until bubbly and golden.
  9. Let rest for 15 minutes before serving.
  10. Garnish with fresh basil if desired.
